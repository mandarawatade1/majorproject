## Circular DEMO/IT/2026/02

**To:** All staff and students
**From:** IT Services
**Subject:** Planned maintenance, campus network core

## Window

Maintenance is scheduled for **Sunday 22 March 2026, 06:00 to 14:00**. The
window is chosen to fall outside teaching hours. Work may finish earlier; the
window will not be extended without a further notice.

## What will be unavailable

- Campus wired and wireless network, including guest access
- Student portal and staff portal
- Library digital resources reached through the campus proxy
- Internal file shares and departmental lab servers
- Printing and print quota services

## What will remain available

- Institutional email, which is externally hosted
- Any service reached from outside the campus network that does not use the
  campus proxy

## Why

The core switch firmware is being upgraded and the wireless controller is being
migrated to new hardware. Both require the network to be down; neither can be
done in a rolling fashion on the current topology.

## Before the window

Staff and students are advised to: download any material needed for Sunday
before Saturday evening; avoid scheduling online assessments or viva sessions in
the window; and save and close files held on departmental shares before Saturday
18:00, since unsaved work on a share will not survive the shutdown.

Any laboratory session scheduled in the window must be rescheduled by the
department concerned. IT Services will not be able to support lab activity
during the work.

## After the window

Devices that were connected before the maintenance may need to reconnect to the
wireless network and re-authenticate. Where a device does not reconnect, follow
DEMO/HELP/WIFI/2026/01 before raising a ticket.

## Contact

Issues persisting after 14:00 on 22 March should be raised as tickets through
the helpdesk. Tickets raised during the window itself will be queued and not
acted on until the work is complete.
