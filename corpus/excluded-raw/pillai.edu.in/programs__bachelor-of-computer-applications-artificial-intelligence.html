    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is eligibility for BCA AI at Pillai University—Is Maths required in Class 12, minimum percentage, and accepted entrance exams?

    Eligibility for BCA – Artificial Intelligence at Pillai University: Students must have minimum 70% in Class 12 with Mathematics as a compulsory subject, and admission is based on PULSE, MAH-CET, or CUET entrance exam scores.

    What is the difference between BCA AI, regular BCA, and B.Tech CSE AI—which is best for an AI career?

    BCA with AI focuses on practical AI tools and industry-oriented skills, while regular BCA covers general computer applications. B.Tech CSE with AI offers deeper technical and mathematical foundations. For AI careers, B.Tech provides strong technical expertise, whereas BCA with AI is a good application-focused pathway into the industry.

    What will I learn in BCA AI at Pillai University—Python, ML, Neural Networks, Full Stack, Cloud AI, NLP, or Generative AI?

    In BCA Artificial Intelligence at Pillai University, students gain hands-on experience in Python programming, Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Full Stack Development, Cloud-based AI tools, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and emerging technologies like Generative AI, supported by practical projects and industry-oriented learning.

    What is the fee structure for BCA Artificial Intelligence at Pillai University, and are scholarships available?

    The fee for BCA Artificial Intelligence at Pillai University is approximately ₹1.5 lakhs per year, and eligible students may apply for scholarships as per university norms.

    Is BCA with Artificial Intelligence worth choosing over B.Tech CSE in 2026 — what are the differences in career scope, salary, and industry recognition?

    BCA with Artificial Intelligence is a great choice for students who want practical AI, programming, and industry-oriented skills in a shorter and more application-focused program, while B.Tech CSE with AI is a more engineering-intensive course with deeper theoretical and mathematical foundations. Both offer strong career opportunities in AI and tech, but BCA AI provides a faster, skill-driven pathway into the industry.

    What are top career roles after BCA AI—AI Full Stack Developer, ML Developer, AI Application Developer, Data Analyst, or Cloud AI Engineer?

    BCA Artificial Intelligence, students can build careers in high-demand roles such as AI Full Stack Developer, Machine Learning (ML) Developer, AI Application Developer, Data Analyst, and Cloud AI Engineer. With strong skills in Python, AI tools, cloud platforms, and real-world projects, graduates are well prepared for opportunities in the rapidly growing AI and technology industry.

    What is the average starting salary after BCA Artificial Intelligence in India — and how does it grow with experience?

    The AI industry offers strong salary growth The average starting salary after BCA Artificial Intelligence in India ranges between ₹4–8 LPA, depending on skills, internships, and projects. With experience in AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud technologies, professionals can grow to ₹10–20 LPA or higher within a few years

    Which top tech companies hire BCA Artificial Intelligence graduates — TCS, Infosys, Wipro, startups, or global MNCs like Google and Microsoft?

    BCA Artificial Intelligence graduates can find opportunities in top IT companies such as TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Capgemini, and IBM, as well as global tech companies like Amazon, Google, and Microsoft, along with emerging AI startups.

    Does the BCA AI program at Pillai University include a mandatory internship, real-world AI capstone project, and industry-recognised certifications?

    Yes, the BCA Artificial Intelligence program at Pillai University includes industry-oriented learning with a semester internship, real-world AI capstone projects, and opportunities to earn industry-recognised certifications, helping students gain practical experience and strong industry exposure.

    What higher study options exist after BCA AI—MCA AI, M.Tech CSE, MBA Data Science, MS AI abroad, or certifications from Google, AWS, or Microsoft?

    After BCA AI, students can pursue MCA (AI/Data Science) for technical roles, M.Tech for advanced research, MBA (Analytics/IT) for management careers, or MS abroad for global opportunities. They can also boost careers with certifications from Google, AWS, and Microsoft. Choice depends on goals—technical specialization, leadership roles, or international exposure.
