The National Board of Accreditation (NBA) has accredited the following programs at Pillai College of Engineering:

- Automobile Engineering
- Computer Engineering
- Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering
- Information Technology
- Mechanical Engineering

These programs have been accredited for 3 years, with the latest accreditation extending until June 30, 2025.
