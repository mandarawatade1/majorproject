    BBA International Business Program Overview

    The BBA International Business provides a comprehensive academic foundation with industry-relevant curriculum, practical learning, and career-focused education designed for professional success.

    The program develops foundational business knowledge with a strong global orientation, emphasizing international trade, cross-cultural management, global markets, and supply chain dynamics to prepare students for multinational business environments.

    Students learn global business principles, international trade practices, cross-cultural communication, market analysis, supply chain coordination, and strategic decision-making using analytical, managerial, and economic frameworks.

    To apply for the BBA International Business programme at Pillai University, one of the top private universities in Mumbai, candidates must meet the following criteria:

    BBA International Business Eligibility:

    Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Higher Secondary), securing a minimum of 60% aggregate (as per university norms).

    BBA International Business Entrance Exams:

    A valid score in PULSE, CUET, SET, MET, NPAT or other recognised national/state-level tests is required. These exams are used for merit-based selection into the programme.

    BBA International Business Application Process:

    Applications are submitted online through the university portal. Shortlisted candidates may be called for counselling or personal interaction as per university guidelines.

    BBA International Business Duration & Semesters:

    The programme runs for 3 degree/4 honors years, comprising 6 semesters.

    BBA International Business Fee:

    Approximately ₹2,75,000 per annum.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the eligibility criteria for BBA International Business at Pillai University — minimum percentage, accepted streams, and entrance exams?

    To apply for BBA International Business at Pillai University, students must have passed Class 12 from any stream with around 60% marks. Admission is based on entrance exams like PULSE.

    What is the difference between BBA International Business and a regular BBA — which one offers better global career prospects?

    BBA International Business focuses on global trade and international markets, while BBA covers general business. IB is better for global career opportunities.

    What subjects and skills are covered in the BBA International Business curriculum — International Trade, Cross-Cultural Management, Global Supply Chains, Export-Import, or Geopolitics?

    The BBA International Business curriculum covers subjects like International Trade, Cross-Cultural Management, Global Supply Chains, Export-Import management, and global business environment.

    What is the fee structure for BBA International Business at Pillai University, and are scholarships available?

    The BBA International Business fee at Pillai University is about ₹2.75lakh per year, and scholarships are available as per the university policy

    Does the BBA International Business program include foreign language training — French, German, Spanish, or Mandarin?

    Yes, the BBA International Business program includes foreign language training such as French, German, Spanish, or Mandarin to support global business careers.

    What are the top career roles and average salary after BBA International Business — Export Manager, International Marketing Executive, Trade Compliance Officer?

    After BBA International Business, students can work in roles like Export Manager, International Marketing Executive, Trade Compliance Officer, or Global Supply Chain Manager, with starting salaries around ₹7LPA.

    Can a BBA International Business graduate work at top MNCs, embassies, global consulting firms, or international trade bodies like DGFT and WTO?

    Yes, BBA International Business graduates can work with MNCs, embassies, consulting firms, and trade bodies like the Directorate General of Foreign Trade and World Trade Organization.

    Does the program include mandatory international internships, global case studies, or exchange programs with foreign universities?

    Yes, the program includes international internships, global case studies, and exchange opportunities with foreign universities to provide global exposure.

    Is BBA International Business a good choice in 2026, given global trade disruptions, deglobalisation trends, and the rise of digital commerce?

    Yes, BBA International Business is a good choice in 2026 as global trade and digital commerce continue to create demand for professionals who understand international markets.

    What are the higher study options after BBA International Business — MBA in International Business, Global MBA abroad, PGDM in Supply Chain?

    After BBA International Business, students can pursue MBA in International Business, Global MBA abroad, PGDM in Supply Chain, or MS in International Trade.
