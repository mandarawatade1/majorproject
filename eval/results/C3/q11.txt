Students have access to a variety of library resources, including:

- **E-Resources**: Students can access IEEE Explorer Digital Library, which includes 336 journals. [S2]
- **Aggregated Open Access Resources**: The library has a collection of open education resources, open courses, online free databases, and lecture videos. [S3]
- **Physical Books and Materials**: The library has a large collection of books, journals, periodicals, project theses, CDs, multimedia CDs, and newspapers. [S4]
- **Component Library**: For practical projects, students can check out components like sensors, motors, IoT devices, Raspberry Pis, and Arduino boards. [S1]
- **Plagiarism Software**: The library uses Turnitin for plagiarism detection. [S4]

These resources are designed to support both teaching and learning activities. [S4]
