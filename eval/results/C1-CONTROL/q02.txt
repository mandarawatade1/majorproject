The Computer Engineering department at Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel, offers a Ph.D. program. This program provides post-master’s applicants with the opportunity to study in a broad range of areas, including but not limited to Computer Networks, Data Mining, Image Processing, Cyber Security & Digital Forensics, Natural Language Processing, Ubiquitous Computing, Machine Learning and Data Science, Internet of Things, and Big Data Analytics.
