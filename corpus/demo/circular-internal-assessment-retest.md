## Circular DEMO/EXAM/2026/03

**To:** All students and faculty
**From:** Examination Office
**Subject:** Eligibility and procedure for internal assessment retest

## Who is eligible

A retest is offered only where a student missed a scheduled internal assessment
for one of the following reasons, supported by documentation:

1. **Medical.** Illness or injury, supported by a medical certificate covering
   the date of the assessment, submitted within seven days of the assessment.
2. **Institutional representation.** Participation in a sanctioned sporting,
   cultural or technical event representing the institute, with the sanction
   letter attached.
3. **Bereavement.** Death of an immediate family member, on written intimation.
4. **Documented emergency.** Considered case by case by the Head of Department,
   whose decision is recorded with reasons.

## Who is not eligible

A retest is not offered for: poor performance in an assessment that was
attempted; travel or personal arrangements; overlapping commitments that were
known in advance and not raised in advance; or missing the assessment because
the schedule was not read. The schedule is published at the start of term.

A student who has already been granted a retest for the same course in the same
semester is not eligible for a second one.

## Applying

Apply on Form DEMO/EXAM/F2 within **seven working days** of the missed
assessment, attaching documentation. Applications outside this window are not
considered, however strong the reason, because the retest schedule is fixed
shortly after.

The application is verified by the course teacher, recommended by the Head of
Department, and sanctioned by the Examination Office.

## Conduct of the retest

Retests are held in a single consolidated window in the week following the
regular assessment. The syllabus, duration and marks are identical to the
regular assessment; the question paper is different.

A student who applies, is granted a retest, and then does not appear is marked
absent with no further opportunity.

## Marks

Retest marks replace the missed assessment's marks in full. There is no cap and
no penalty, since the retest exists to remove disadvantage rather than to
create it. Marks are published with the regular assessment marks for the course.

## Records

The examination office maintains the retest register recording the student, the
course, the ground, the sanctioning authority, and the date. Aggregate retest
counts by ground are reviewed at the end of each semester.
