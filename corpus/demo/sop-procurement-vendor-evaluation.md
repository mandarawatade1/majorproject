> Threshold values in this document are **illustrative placeholders** used to
> exercise the system. They are not real delegation limits.

## 1. Purpose

To define how equipment and consumables are procured, how many quotations are
required at each value band, and how vendors are evaluated.

## 2. Value bands and approval

| Band | Illustrative value | Quotations required | Approving authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Up to one unit | 1 | Head of Department |
| B | Above one, up to five units | 3 | Head of Department + Administrative Officer |
| C | Above five, up to twenty units | 3 sealed | Purchase Committee |
| D | Above twenty units | Open tender | Purchase Committee + Principal |

"Unit" is a placeholder denomination. The point of the table is the escalation
structure, not the numbers.

## 3. Splitting is prohibited

A requirement is not split into smaller orders to bring it under a lower band.
Two requisitions for the same item from the same department within one quarter
are treated as a single requirement and re-banded. This is checked at
reconciliation, not at raising, so splitting is detected after the fact.

## 4. Requisition

Form DEMO/FIN/F4 records: the item with specification, quantity, purpose, the
budget line, whether an equivalent exists elsewhere in the department, and the
expected life. The "equivalent exists" field is not a formality — shared
equipment sitting idle in one lab while another buys the same thing is the most
common avoidable spend.

## 5. Quotations

Quotations must be for the same specification. A cheaper quotation against a
lower specification is not a comparison and is returned. Where only one vendor
can supply, a single-source justification is recorded with reasons.

Quotations older than ninety days are not accepted.

## 6. Vendor evaluation

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Price | 40 |
| Specification compliance | 25 |
| Delivery timeline | 15 |
| Warranty and support terms | 10 |
| Past performance with the institute | 10 |

Lowest price does not win automatically. A vendor with a recorded history of
late delivery or poor support is scored accordingly, and the committee records
its reasoning where it does not select the lowest quotation.

## 7. Receipt and asset registration

Goods are inspected against the specification on receipt by the indenting
department, not by the office that raised the payment. Equipment is entered in
the asset register with an asset tag before it is put into service — see
DEMO/SOP/LAB/2026/04 for the tag format.

Payment is released after inspection and asset registration, never before.

## 8. Records

The purchase register holds requisition, quotations, evaluation, order,
receipt and payment against one reference number, so any purchase can be
reconstructed end to end.
