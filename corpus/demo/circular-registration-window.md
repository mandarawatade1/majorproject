## Circular DEMO/ACAD/2026/11

**To:** All students
**From:** Academic Office
**Subject:** Registration for the odd semester, and the late window

> Fee amounts below are **illustrative placeholders**, not real charges.

## Windows

| Window | Dates | Late fee |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | 1 to 12 July 2026 | None |
| Late | 13 to 20 July 2026 | Illustrative flat charge |
| Exceptional | After 20 July 2026 | Illustrative charge + HoD approval |

Registration closes entirely on 31 July 2026. There is no registration after
that date in any circumstance; the enrolment list is transmitted to the
examination office on 1 August.

## Before you start

Registration passes five gates in order — programme standing, financial
clearance, library clearance, academic eligibility, and course selection
validity. Two of these do not resolve by waiting.

If registration fails, establish which gate blocked you before retrying.
Repeatedly clicking Register will not clear a programme-record or eligibility
block. See DEMO/WIKI/ACAD/031, which staff use to diagnose these.

## Fee payments clear overnight

The fee record updates in an overnight batch. A payment made today typically
clears the financial gate tomorrow. Students paying on the last day of the
regular window will therefore usually register in the late window and pay the
late fee. Pay early.

## Electives

Electives are allocated first-come within capacity. Capacity is counted on
**completed** registrations only — an incomplete registration holds no
reservation. Complete registration in one sitting rather than leaving it part
done and returning later.

## Backlogs

Students carrying backlogs beyond the permitted number are blocked at the
academic eligibility gate and cannot register through the portal. This is not
a portal fault. Approach the academic office; the matter requires a decision,
not a retry.

## Confirmation

Registration is complete when the portal shows a registration number and the
course list. A payment receipt is not a registration. Students who have paid
but not completed course selection are not registered, and this is the single
most common misunderstanding each semester.

## Queries

Academic office during working hours. Bring your identity number and, if
registration failed, the exact message shown.
