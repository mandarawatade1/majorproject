## Why this exists

Accreditation requires the institute to show that stated course outcomes are
actually attained, and how course attainment rolls up into programme outcomes.
This page documents the method so that every department computes it the same
way. Departments computing it differently produce numbers that cannot be
aggregated, which is the failure this page prevents.

## Definitions

**CO** — Course Outcome. What a student should be able to do on completing a
course. Typically four to six per course.

**PO** — Programme Outcome. The graduate attributes the programme claims.

**CO-PO mapping** — the strength with which a CO contributes to a PO, recorded
as 3 (strong), 2 (moderate), 1 (weak) or blank (none).

## Attainment from direct assessment

For each CO, the attainment level is computed from the proportion of students
reaching the target mark on the questions mapped to that CO.

| Proportion of students at or above target | Attainment level |
|---|---|
| 70% and above | 3 |
| 60% to 69.9% | 2 |
| 50% to 59.9% | 1 |
| Below 50% | 0 |

The target mark is 60% of the marks allotted to the mapped questions unless the
course teacher records a different target with reasons before the assessment.
Setting the target after seeing the results is not permitted, for obvious
reasons.

## Direct and indirect

Overall CO attainment weights direct assessment at 80% and indirect assessment —
course-end student feedback — at 20%. Indirect assessment alone never
establishes attainment; it contextualises it.

## Rolling up to PO

A PO's attainment is the weighted mean of the attainment of every CO mapped to
it, weighted by the mapping strength. A CO mapped at strength 3 contributes
three times as much as one mapped at strength 1.

Mapping every CO to every PO at strength 3 makes the roll-up meaningless. Maps
should be sparse and honest; a course that does not develop a PO should leave
it blank.

## Gap and action

A PO attaining below the target level triggers a documented action for the next
cycle: revised delivery, revised assessment, or a revised map where the original
map was optimistic. The action is recorded against the PO and reviewed the
following year, closing the loop.

An attainment report with no actions where gaps exist is incomplete and is
returned by the IQAC.

## Records

Each department holds, per course per semester: the CO statements, the CO-PO
map, the question-to-CO mapping for each assessment, the computed attainment,
and the actions arising.
