    Our History

    Founded in 1970, the Pillai Group of Institutions (Mahatma Education Society - MES) has evolved into a distinguished, multi-disciplinary educational network committed to excellence in academics, research, and holistic development. Over the years, it has expanded into a premier cluster of institutions offering programs in engineering, management, commerce, arts, science, architecture, and various professional fields.

    Today, we stand as a multi-disciplinary educational powerhouse, recognized for our world-class faculty, cutting-edge research, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and industry-integrated learning environment. With a strong focus on innovation, inclusivity, and global readiness, it prepares students to excel in an ever-evolving world.

    Our Vision & Mission

    Our Vision

    • To become an institution fostering academic excellence, innovation, and leadership through education, cutting-edge research, having a societal impact, while nurturing employability, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning.

    Our Mission

    • Pillai University shall be committed to:

      • Delivering high-quality, multidisciplinary education that equips students with industry-relevant skills and global competencies.
      • Promoting a culture of innovation, research, and entrepreneurship to address societal challenges and advance economic development.
      • Fostering an inclusive learning environment that values diversity, creativity, and sustainability.
      • Building meaningful partnerships with industries, academic institutions, and government agencies to enhance learning, research, and employability.
