The undergraduate and postgraduate Computer Engineering programs differ in both duration and eligibility requirements:

1. **Duration:**
   - **Undergraduate Programs:** These typically last for 4 years. Examples include Computer Engineering at the Bachelor's degree level, such as Computer Engineering, Electronics & Computer Science, and Information Technology.
   - **Postgraduate Programs:** These programs are shorter, ranging from 2 to 3 years. For instance, the Master's degree in Computer Engineering and the Ph.D. program in Computer Engineering both last for 2 and 3 years, respectively.

2. **Eligibility:**
   - **Undergraduate Programs:** Generally, eligibility for these programs requires a 10+2 qualification with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or equivalent.
   - **Postgraduate Programs:** For Master's programs, a Bachelor's degree in a relevant field is typically required. For the Ph.D. program, a Master's degree or equivalent is necessary, along with a strong academic record and research potential.

These differences highlight the progression from foundational to advanced studies in the field of Computer Engineering.
