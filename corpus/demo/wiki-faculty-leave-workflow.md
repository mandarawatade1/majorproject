## Scope

Applies to teaching staff. Non-teaching leave follows the administrative
office's own procedure.

## Categories

| Category | Notice expected | Class adjustment required |
|---|---|---|
| Casual leave | 2 working days | Yes |
| Medical leave | As soon as practicable | Yes, by the department |
| Duty leave (conference, examination duty) | 10 working days | Yes |
| Compensatory off | 5 working days | Yes |
| Long leave | 30 working days | Course reallocation, not adjustment |

## The rule that causes most of the friction

**Leave is not approved until class adjustment is recorded.** An application
with no adjustment plan sits pending, and the applicant often believes it is
approved because they submitted it. The adjustment plan is part of the
application, not a follow-up to it.

Adjustment means a named colleague has agreed to take the session, or the
session is formally rescheduled with the students informed. "Students will be
given an assignment" is not an adjustment.

## Applying

1. Applicant records the leave in the staff portal with dates, category and
   reason.
2. Applicant records the adjustment plan: for each affected session, the
   substituting colleague or the rescheduled slot.
3. Substituting colleagues confirm on the portal. An unconfirmed substitution
   does not count.
4. Head of Department approves, subject to departmental coverage.
5. For long leave and for leave crossing a month boundary, the Principal's
   office approves.

## Duty leave

Duty leave requires the sanction letter or invitation attached at the point of
application, not afterwards. Duty leave taken without prior sanction is
converted to casual leave on reconciliation, which is a poor surprise at the end
of the year.

## Medical leave

Where illness prevents advance notice, the applicant or a colleague informs the
Head of Department by any means on the first day. The formal application and
certificate follow on return. The department arranges adjustment; the absent
member is not expected to arrange it from a sick bed.

Medical leave beyond three consecutive days requires a certificate. Beyond
fifteen days it is treated as long leave and triggers course reallocation.

## Examination periods

Leave during examination duty periods is granted only for medical or emergency
grounds. Duty rosters are published in advance precisely so that other
commitments can be planned around them.

## Records

The leave register reconciles against attendance monthly. Discrepancies —
attendance marked present on a sanctioned leave day, or absence with no
application — are raised with the Head of Department in the monthly note.
