## 1. Purpose

To define how similarity checking is carried out on project reports,
dissertations and publishable manuscripts, what the thresholds mean, and how
exceedances are handled.

## 2. What is checked

Every major project report, every M.Tech dissertation, and every manuscript
submitted for publication under the institute's affiliation is checked before
final submission. Mini-project reports are checked at the supervisor's
discretion.

Bibliography, correctly quoted material with attribution, and standard
institutional front matter (certificate, declaration, approval page) are
excluded from the similarity computation. Including them inflates the figure
and makes the threshold meaningless.

## 3. Thresholds

| Similarity index | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Acceptable. Proceed to submission. |
| 11% to 25% | Return to the student for revision. One re-check permitted. |
| 26% to 40% | Return for substantial revision. Supervisor's written note required. Re-check mandatory. |
| Above 40% | Referred to the Academic Integrity Committee. |

A single source contributing more than 10% on its own is escalated regardless
of the overall index, because a low total can conceal one heavily copied
section.

## 4. Process

The supervisor initiates the check at least **fifteen working days** before the
submission deadline. The report is submitted as a single file in a text-bearing
format; a scanned or image-only PDF is rejected, because it cannot be checked.

The generated report, showing the overall index and the source breakdown, is
retained with the project record. It is shared with the student, since a student
cannot revise against a figure they have not seen.

## 5. Re-checks

One re-check is permitted at the 11–25% band. Beyond that, further checks
require the Head of Department to record a reason. Repeated re-submission
without substantive revision is itself reported to the Committee.

## 6. Referral to the Academic Integrity Committee

The Committee reviews the report, the sources and the student's written
explanation. It may require revision and re-submission, may require the work to
be redone, or in a serious and deliberate case may recommend action under the
institute's disciplinary rules. The student is heard before any finding is
recorded.

## 7. Records

The examination office holds, for each submission: the similarity index, the
date of check, the number of re-checks, and the outcome. Aggregate figures
without student names are reported annually to the IQAC.
