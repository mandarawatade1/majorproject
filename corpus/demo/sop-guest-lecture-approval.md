## 1. Purpose

To define the approval path, lead times and record-keeping for guest lectures,
expert sessions and industry talks organised by a department or a student
association.

## 2. Minimum lead time

| Session type | Minimum notice before the date |
|---|---|
| Internal speaker, no honorarium | 7 working days |
| External speaker, no honorarium | 15 working days |
| External speaker with honorarium or travel | 21 working days |
| Speaker from outside the country | 30 working days |

Proposals arriving inside the minimum notice are not automatically refused, but
require the Head of Department to record a written reason for the exception.

## 3. Proposal

The organising faculty member or association convener submits Form
DEMO/ACAD/F7 with: proposed date and duration, speaker name and affiliation, a
short speaker profile, topic and its mapping to a course outcome or a stated
skill gap, expected attendance, venue required, and the full expected cost
broken into honorarium, travel and refreshments.

The course-outcome mapping is not a formality. Sessions that cannot be mapped
to a course outcome or an identified skill gap are the first to be questioned
when the annual activity report is compiled.

## 4. Approval chain

1. **Faculty coordinator** checks the academic fit and that the date does not
   clash with assessments.
2. **Head of Department** approves the academic case and the departmental
   budget line.
3. **Administrative office** confirms venue availability and, where a cost is
   involved, budget sanction.
4. **Principal's office** approves only where an honorarium exceeds the
   departmental delegation or the speaker is from outside the country.

Each approver records approval against the form's reference number. Verbal
approval does not advance the proposal to the next stage.

## 5. Before the session

The organiser confirms the speaker in writing at least five working days ahead,
arranges the venue and equipment, and circulates the announcement through the
department notice board and the student portal. Where attendance is to be
counted toward a student's activity record, the announcement must say so in
advance.

## 6. After the session

Within **five working days** the organiser submits: the attendance sheet, at
least two photographs, a feedback summary if feedback was collected, and a
brief report of what was covered. Honorarium payment is released only after
this submission, which is the mechanism that keeps the activity record complete.

## 7. Records

The departmental activity register holds one row per session with its reference
number, date, speaker, topic, attendance and mapped course outcome. This
register is the source for the department's contribution to the annual report,
so an unrecorded session is, for reporting purposes, a session that did not
happen.
