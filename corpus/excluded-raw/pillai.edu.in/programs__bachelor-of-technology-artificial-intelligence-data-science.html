    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the eligibility criteria for B.Tech AI & Data Science at Pillai University?

    Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics, along with one of Chemistry, Computer Science, Biology, or IT, securing at least 45% aggregate. A valid score in JEE Main, CET, PULSE, or other recognized entrance exams is required for merit-based admission.

    What is the difference between B.Tech AI & Data Science and B.Tech CSE?

    B.Tech CSE provides a broad foundation in programming, software development, algorithms, and areas like AI and data science. B.Tech AI & Data Science is more specialized, focusing on machine learning, deep learning, and data engineering. Choose CSE for broader career options, or AI & Data Science for a focused path in intelligent systems and analytics.

    What specialization tracks are available in the AI & Data Science program?

    1) Cognitive Computing & Intelligence: Covers Generative AI, LLMs, Computer Vision, and NLP. 2) Cyber Security & Cloud Defense: Focuses on blockchain, cryptography, and ethical hacking. 3) IoT & Data Analytics: Combines 5G, smart automation, and social media analytics. 4) Data Management: Includes big data, cloud data engineering, and business intelligence.

    What is the fee structure and scholarship availability for B.Tech AI & Data Science?

    Fees for B.Tech AI & Data Science is Rs. 3,50,000/-. Upto 100% merit based scholarships are available.

    Is B.Tech in AI & Data Science worth it in 2026?

    B.Tech in AI & Data Science will remain valuable in 2026 as industries increasingly rely on data-driven technologies and intelligent systems. Despite automation, skilled professionals are needed to design models, manage data, and improve AI systems, ensuring strong career demand.Even with automation, skilled professionals are needed to design models, manage data, and improve AI systems, ensuring strong career demand.

    What programming languages and tools are covered in this program?

    Students learn C, Python, Java, and SQL, along with tools like Tableau, Power BI, Hadoop, and Spark. The curriculum also includes frameworks such as TensorFlow/Keras, Generative AI, and cloud engineering for full-stack technical skills.

    What is the average salary after B.Tech AI & Data Science?

    For freshers, salaries typically range from ₹6–12 LPA, with top graduates earning ₹15–25+ LPA. Professionals with 5–10 years of experience can earn ₹25–50+ LPA, especially in roles like AI Architects and Data Science Leads.

    What are the top job roles after B.Tech AI & Data Science?

    1) AI/ML Engineer: Works on intelligent systems (Cognitive Computing). 2) Data Scientist: Focuses on data analysis and modeling (Data Management). 3) Business Analyst: Uses data insights for decisions (IoT & Data Analytics). 4) Cyber Security Specialist: Secures systems and data (Cyber Security).

    Does the program include AI Ethics, Responsible AI, and Data Privacy?

    Yes, the program integrates Ethics and Human Values along with a Cyber Security vertical covering Cyber Laws and Ethical Hacking. This helps students understand regulations like the DPDP Act and build secure, unbiased, and legally compliant AI systems for sectors such as finance and healthcare.

    What are the higher study options after B.Tech AI & Data Science?

    Graduates can pursue an M.Tech via GATE Data Science and AI (DA) for research in India, or an MS Abroad for global specializations like Robotics. Alternatively, an MBA in Business Analytics prepares for leadership, while Industry Certifications (AWS/Google) offer a fast track to mastering GenAI and Cloud Engineering.
