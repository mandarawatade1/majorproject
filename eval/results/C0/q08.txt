Computer Engineering students at Pillai College of Engineering have access to a variety of laboratory facilities designed to enhance their learning and research experience. These include:

1. **Undergraduate Labs:**
   - Open Source Lab
   - Computer Architecture Lab
   - Advanced Database Lab
   - Computer Programming and Data Structure Lab
   - Network Lab
   - Web Engineering Lab
   - Database and Data Mining Lab
   - System Software Lab
   - Big Data Lab
   - Artificial Intelligence Lab

2. **Postgraduate Lab:**
   - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Lab

Additionally, the college offers specialized research centers and labs, such as:

- **High Performance Computing Lab:** Equipped with advanced hardware like AMD Thread Ripper (2nd Gen) 2970WX and Nvidia RTX 3090 24 GB GPU, this lab supports research in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Lab:** Also funded by AICTE under MODROB's Scheme, this lab provides state-of-the-art infrastructure for students to work on projects related to AI and ML.

These facilities cater to both undergraduate and postgraduate students, providing them with the necessary tools and resources to excel in their studies and research.
