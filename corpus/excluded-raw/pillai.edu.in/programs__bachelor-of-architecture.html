    Bachelor of Architecture Program Overview

    The Bachelor of Architecture provides a comprehensive academic foundation with industry-relevant curriculum, practical learning, and career-focused education designed for professional success.

    The Bachelor of Architecture is a five-year Council of Architecture–approved program integrating design studios, technical knowledge, and professional practice to develop creative, responsible architects capable of addressing social, environmental, and spatial challenges.

    Students learn architectural design methodologies, building technologies, structural and environmental systems, urban and spatial planning principles, professional practice, and research-driven design thinking through studio work, field exposure, and guided internships.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the eligibility criteria for B.Arch at Pillai University?

    Candidates must have at least 45% aggregate in 10+2 or 10+3. A valid score in NATA or JEE Main Paper 2 is required for admission.

    What is the difference between B.Arch and B.Tech Civil Engineering?

    B.Arch focuses on designing buildings and spaces with creativity and technology. B.Tech Civil Engineering focuses on structural design, calculations, and construction execution. Choose Architecture if you enjoy creative design, or Civil Engineering if you prefer calculations and site work.

    What is the fee structure and scholarship availability for B.Arch at Pillai University?

    The fee for B.Arch is Rs. 2,50,000 per year for 5 years. Scholarships are available for eligible students based on merit and university policy.

    What does the B.Arch curriculum at Pillai University cover?

    The curriculum includes areas like Sustainable Architecture, Smart Cities, Urban Design, Heritage Conservation, and BIM (Building Information Modelling).

    Does the B.Arch program include internship and thesis, and when do they occur?

    Yes, internship is compulsory in Semester 8. Thesis work begins with topic selection in Semester 7 and is carried out in Semesters 9 and 10 as part of the 5-year program.

    Is B.Arch a good career choice in India in 2026?

    Yes, B.Arch is a strong career choice due to growing opportunities in Smart Cities, housing projects, green building initiatives, and infrastructure development.

    What is the average salary after B.Arch in India?

    Entry-level salaries range from ₹30,000–35,000 per month. With 5–6 years of experience, salaries can reach ₹80,000–1 lakh per month. Senior professionals (15+ years) can earn ₹40 LPA or more, depending on expertise and skills.

    What are the top career roles after B.Arch?

    Architectural Designer, Interior Designer, Urban Planner, Landscape Architect, Heritage Conservationist, BIM Specialist.

    What software and design tools are taught in the B.Arch program?

    Students learn tools like AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, Rhino, 3ds Max, Lumion, and Grasshopper, along with AR/VR technologies.

    What are the higher study options after B.Arch?

    Graduates can pursue M.Arch, Urban Design, Landscape Architecture, or other related master’s programs in India or abroad by qualifying the required entrance exams.
