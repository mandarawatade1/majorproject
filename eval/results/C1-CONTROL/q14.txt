3. **Industrial Visits and Field Trips**: During such visits, attendance is recorded at multiple points to ensure that any student who is absent is identified early. 4. **Computation of Attendance**: Attendance is computed from the recorded sessions in the registration grid, up to the cut-off date published each semester. Sessions cancelled by the institute are excluded from the denominator, and missed sessions by the student are included. A rescheduled session is counted against its original slot, emphasizing the need for formal recording of leave adjustments (SOP for Attendance Shortage, Warning and Detention).

5. **Thresholds and Actions**: The thresholds for attendance are defined as follows:
   - 75% and above: Satisfactory, no action required.
   - 65% to 74.9%: First warning, with a written warning and mentor informed.
   - 55% to 64.9%: Second warning, with parental/guardian involvement and a mentor meeting.
   - Below 55%: Detention risk, referred to the detention committee.
   Attendance is computed per course, not as an overall average, to ensure that students who are performing well in most courses but poorly in one are identified and supported (SOP for Attendance Shortage, Warning and Detention).

These policies are designed to maintain high standards of attendance and academic performance while providing necessary support and interventions for students facing challenges.
