The undergraduate (Bachelor of Technology) Computer Engineering program at Pillai College of Engineering has a duration of 4 years [S4]. For postgraduate studies, the Master of Technology (M.Tech) program in Computer Engineering lasts for 2 years [S4]. The eligibility for the undergraduate program typically requires a qualifying degree, while the postgraduate program is open to those who have completed a bachelor's degree and meet the necessary academic criteria [S4].
