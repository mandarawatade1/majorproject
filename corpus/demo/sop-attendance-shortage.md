## 1. Thresholds

| Attendance | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 75% and above | Satisfactory | None |
| 65% to 74.9% | First warning | Written warning; mentor informed |
| 55% to 64.9% | Second warning | Parent/guardian informed; mentor meeting |
| Below 55% | Detention risk | Referred to the detention committee |

Attendance is computed **per course**, not as an overall average. A student at
85% overall but 52% in one course is at risk in that course. Averaging across
courses conceals exactly the case this procedure exists to catch.

## 2. Computation

Attendance is computed from the recorded sessions in the registration grid, up
to the cut-off date published each semester. Sessions cancelled by the
institute are excluded from the denominator; sessions the student missed are
not.

A rescheduled session is counted against its original slot, which is why leave
adjustments must be recorded rather than handled informally — see
DEMO/WIKI/HR/008.

## 3. Warning stages

Warnings are issued at the published review points, not continuously. A student
crossing a threshold between review points is caught at the next one.

Each warning is recorded against the student, communicated in writing, and
copied to the assigned mentor. A warning that is issued but not recorded does
not exist for the purposes of the detention committee, which reviews the
recorded history.

## 4. Condonation

Attendance may be condoned for: documented illness, institutional
representation, or a documented emergency, on the same evidentiary basis as
DEMO/EXAM/2026/03.

Condonation is applied to specific dates, never as a blanket percentage
adjustment. The condoned sessions are marked as such and remain visible in the
record; the computation excludes them from the denominator.

Condonation is not available for attendance lost to routine personal
commitments, travel, or unawareness of the schedule.

## 5. Detention committee

The committee reviews each referred student's per-course record, the warnings
issued, any condonation granted, and the student's written explanation. The
student is heard before a decision.

The committee may: permit the student to appear with a recorded caution, permit
appearance conditional on a make-up requirement, or detain the student in the
course.

## 6. Communication

A detention decision is communicated in writing to the student and to the
parent or guardian, with the per-course figures and the warning history
attached. A decision communicated without the figures behind it is not
actionable and invites appeal on procedural grounds.

## 7. Records

The academic office retains per-course attendance, warnings issued,
condonation applied and committee outcomes for the duration of the programme.
