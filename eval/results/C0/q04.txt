The eligibility criteria for admission to the B.Tech programme at Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel, as per the provided context, are as follows:

For Maharashtra State Candidature Candidates:
- The candidate must be an Indian National.
- The candidate must have passed the HSC or its equivalent examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of the following subjects: Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Technical Vocational subject, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices, Agriculture, or Engineering Graphics, and Business Studies.
- The candidate must have obtained at least 45% marks (40% for backward class categories, economically weaker section, and persons with disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only) in the above subjects taken together.
- The candidate must have appeared in all the subjects in MHT-CET2022 and should obtain a non-zero score in MHT-CET 2022.
- Alternatively, the candidate must have passed a Diploma in Engineering and Technology and obtained at least 45% marks (40% for backward class categories, economically weaker section, and persons with disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only).

For All India Candidature Candidates, Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir, and Union Territory of Ladakh Migrant Candidature Candidates:
- The candidate must be an Indian National.
- The candidate must have passed the HSC or its equivalent examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of the following subjects: Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Technical Vocational subject, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices, Agriculture, or Engineering Graphics, and Business Studies.
- The candidate must have obtained at least 45% marks (40% for backward class categories, economically weaker section, and persons with disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only) in the above subjects taken together.
- The candidate must have obtained a non-zero positive score in JEE Main (B.E/B.Tech) or must have appeared in all the subjects in MHT-CET 2022 and obtained a non-zero score in MHT-CET 2022. Preference will be given to candidates obtaining a non-zero positive score in JEE Main (B.E/B.Tech) over those who obtained a non-zero score in MHT-CET 2022.
- Alternatively, the candidate must have passed a Diploma in Engineering and Technology and obtained at least 45% marks (40% for backward class categories, economically weaker section, and persons with disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only).
