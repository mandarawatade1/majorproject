## Structure

Every student is allocated a faculty mentor on admission and normally keeps the
same mentor for the whole programme. Continuity is the point: a mentor who has
known a student for three years notices a change that a mentor of three weeks
cannot.

Indicative ratio: **around twenty students per mentor**, distributed across
year groups rather than concentrated in one year.

## Cadence

| Meeting | When | Recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Group meeting | Once per month during term | Attendance only |
| Individual meeting | Twice per semester minimum | Short note |
| Triggered meeting | Within a week of a trigger | Note with the trigger |

## Triggers

A mentor meeting is triggered by: an attendance warning under
DEMO/SOP/ACAD/2026/18, failure in an internal assessment, a retest application,
a registration block that persists more than a week, or a request from the
student.

Triggers are notified to the mentor automatically. A mentor who has not acted
on a trigger within a week is reminded, and after two weeks the Head of
Department is informed. The escalation exists because a student in difficulty
is precisely the student least likely to chase their mentor.

## What is recorded, and what is not

Recorded: that a meeting happened, the general area discussed (academic,
administrative, personal — no detail), and any action agreed.

Not recorded: the content of anything a student shares in confidence about
health, family or personal circumstances. Where such a matter requires action,
the mentor records only that a referral was made and to whom.

Students should be told this at the first meeting. A student who does not know
what is written down will not say anything worth writing down.

## Referral

Mentors are not counsellors and are not expected to be. Where a matter is
beyond academic and administrative guidance, the mentor refers to the
counselling support the administrative office maintains, and follows up only to
confirm the student made contact.

Where a mentor believes a student is at risk, the matter goes to the Head of
Department the same day. This is the one case where confidentiality yields, and
students are told so up front rather than discovering it afterwards.

## Changing mentor

A student may request a change without stating a reason, to the Head of
Department. The request is granted. Requiring a justification defeats the
purpose.

## Records

The mentoring register holds allocations, meeting dates, triggers raised and
whether they were closed. It is reviewed each semester by the Head of
Department, looking for mentors with unclosed triggers rather than for meeting
counts.
