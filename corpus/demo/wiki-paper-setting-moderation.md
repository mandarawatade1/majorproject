## Confidentiality first

Everything in this process is confidential until the paper is used. Setters,
moderators and office staff do not discuss content with anyone outside the
process, including colleagues teaching the same course.

Papers are not drafted or exchanged on personal email or messaging. They move
through the examination office only.

A setter who becomes aware that content may have leaked reports it immediately.
A leak reported early is a paper regenerated; a leak discovered afterwards is an
examination annulled.

## Roles

**Setter.** Drafts the paper and the model answer with a mark scheme. Normally
the course teacher, but not always.

**Moderator.** A second subject expert who checks the paper independently. The
moderator is never the setter and is not from the same course team where that
can be avoided.

**Examination office.** Handles logistics, printing and custody. Does not
review academic content.

## What the moderator checks

1. **Syllabus coverage.** Every unit represented in proportion to its weight.
2. **CO mapping.** Each question mapped to a course outcome, per
   DEMO/WIKI/ACAD/052 — the attainment computation depends on this mapping
   existing before the assessment.
3. **Difficulty distribution.** An indicative split across recall, application
   and analysis rather than a paper that is entirely one of them.
4. **Time feasibility.** Whether the paper is completable in the stated
   duration. A paper that cannot be finished measures speed, not learning.
5. **Ambiguity.** Questions with more than one defensible reading.
6. **Mark scheme.** Whether marks can actually be allocated as stated.

## Sequence

Setter drafts → submits to the examination office → office routes to the
moderator without identifying the setter where practical → moderator returns
comments → setter revises → moderator confirms → office takes custody.

The setter and moderator do not negotiate directly through the office's back.
Where they disagree substantively, the Head of Department decides.

## Timeline

Drafts are submitted **fifteen working days** before the examination.
Moderation completes within five working days. Revision and confirmation take a
further five, leaving five for printing and custody. Compressing this is how
errors reach students.

## After the examination

The model answer and mark scheme go to evaluators unchanged. Where a question
is found to be defective after the paper is used, the matter goes to the
examination committee, which decides on grace marks or exclusion. Individual
evaluators do not make that decision independently, because inconsistent
handling between evaluators is worse than the original defect.

## Records

The examination office retains the paper, the model answer, the moderation
comments and the resolution of any post-examination defect.
