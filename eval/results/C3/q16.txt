The undergraduate (Bachelor of Technology) Computer Engineering program at Pillai College of Engineering has a duration of 4 years [S4]. For postgraduate studies, the Master of Technology (M.Tech.) in Computer Engineering is offered, which lasts for 2 years [S4]. The eligibility for the undergraduate program typically requires a qualifying degree, such as a Bachelor of Technology in a relevant field, while the M.Tech. program is open to those who have completed a Bachelor of Technology or a related field, such as a Bachelor of Engineering [S4].
