    Everything you need to know about Business admissions and life at Pillai University.

    The School of Business offers BBA Finance, BBA International Business, BBA General, Integrated BBA–MBA in Analytics, BSc Aviation Hospitality Management and Bachelor in Sports Management, along with MBA programs
    For BBA Finance, BBA International Business, BBA General and Integrated BBA–MBA in Analytics, eligibility is 10+2 with the required minimum percentage, and students from any stream can apply. Some specialised programs like BSc Aviation Hospitality also accept any stream.
    Most undergraduate business programs accept PULSE (Pillai University’s test) as well as CUET, SET, MET,MAH CET and NPAT. Some niche programs such as BSc Aviation Hospitality Management and Bachelor in Sports Management do not require an entrance exam.
    BBA Finance focuses on financial and analytical skills, BBA International Business on global trade and cross‑border business, BBA General on broad management skills, while Integrated BBA–MBA in Analytics is a 5‑year path blending business with analytics and data‑driven decision making.
    At Pillai university, business program fees range roughly from ₹2–3 lakh per year, and students can avail scholarships like domicile, alumni, merit, and early payment concessions based on eligibility.
    We provide career pathways like Financial Analyst, International Marketing Executive, Business Analyst, Risk Analyst, Export–Import Manager, Investment Banking, Airline Operations, Airport Executive, Sports Operations Manager and ESG & Sustainability Finance Analyst.
    The School of Business showcases strong career pathways, industry‑oriented programs and a startup‑friendly ecosystem, including incubation support for launching ventures, though specific mandatory internship or mentorship structures are detailed within respective program pages.
    Key recruiters such as Deloitte, ICICI, Haldiram, KPMG, HDFC .Adani Airport Holdings Ltd ,Federal bank, Zudio and BSE as industry leaders are engaging with our students.
    For BBA Finance, BBA International Business, BBA General and Integrated BBA–MBA in Analytics, applicants from any 10+2 stream are eligible, subject to the minimum percentage requirement.
    The School of Business emphasises global‑ready, analytics‑aware business education with diverse specialisations, strong career pathways, links with major companies like Deloitte, HDFC and BSE, and an active startup ecosystem with over 100 startups founded through incubation support.
