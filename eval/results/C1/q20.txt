The vision and mission of Pillai College of Engineering (PCE) are as follows:

- **Vision**: Pillai College of Engineering will admit, educate and train a diverse population of students who are academically prepared to benefit from the Institute’s infrastructure and faculty experience, to become responsible professionals or entrepreneurs in a technical arena. [S3, S5]

- **Mission**: To develop professional engineers with respect for the environment and to make them responsible citizens, both from a local and global perspective. This objective is fulfilled through quality education, practical training, research, entrepreneurship and interaction with industries and social organizations. [S1, S5]
