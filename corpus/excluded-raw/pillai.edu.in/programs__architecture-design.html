    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about Architecture & Design admissions and life at Pillai University.

    We offer Bachelor or Architecture (B.Arch), Masters of Architecture (M.Arch), PhD in Architecture, Bachelor in Design, BSc Interior Design, BSc Fashion Design
    Eligibility criteria for all the design streams are different, each is mentioned under its stream. Maths and Physics are not compulsory for Design, Interiors, and Fashion.
    Each course is crafted carefully to bring out the student as the best professional. Architecture deals with designing and executing all kinds of building forms, including landscaping, interiors, urban studies, and town planning the where student can start his/her own practice or opt for government jobs too. B.Des is a creative venture where you explore and become an expert in either product design, visual communication, fashion, interiors etc. Whereas Fashion Designers deal with hard core fashion designing and apparels. Interior Designers are all time favourite where design skills are sharpened to be innovative in designing and executing interior spaces. Each field has its own speciality, it is up to the student's interest and capabilities.
    Fee structure for each course is different, as is also the duration of the course. All details are mentioned in individual stream pages.
    JEE Mains Paper 2 OR NATA - Both exams' scores are valid for B.Arch admission.
    All the design software necessary for each stream will be taught in the curriculum which are clearly mentioned on each design stream pages. Few of them are - AutoCAD, Archi-CAD, Google SketchUp, Revit, Grasshopper, BIM, AR/VR, Adobe Photoshop, InDesign, Python, GIS, BIM, Figma etc.
    Yes, the school offers incubation support for students who want to launch their own design studio or fashion label.
    Since this field allows students to directly start your own firm and your skill sets determine the earning margin after you enter the profession. Each field in Design has different packages again mentioned in the stream pages.
    Definitely, we have compulsory internships and placement cells. We have communication skills and portfolio development as part of our curriculum.
    Students have a well prepared portfolio and software skills. After obtaining a degree, a student has to appear for an appropriate entrance exam to enrol in any of the Masters courses. Also Pillai University has almost all the streams that offer Masters courses.
