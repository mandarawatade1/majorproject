## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how a student grievance is registered, acknowledged,
investigated and closed, and the timeline each stage must meet. It applies to
academic, administrative and facilities grievances. Matters falling under the
Internal Complaints Committee or the Anti-Ragging Committee follow their own
statutory procedures and are outside this document.

## 2. Scope and definitions

A **grievance** is a written expression of dissatisfaction about a service,
decision or facility where the student seeks a remedy. A **query** is a request
for information and is handled at the counter without entering this procedure.
The distinction matters: queries logged as grievances distort the resolution
statistics reported to the department each term.

## 3. Stage 1 — Registration

The student submits the grievance through the student portal under Services →
Grievance, or on Form DEMO/GRV/F1 at the administrative office. Every grievance
receives a reference number of the form `GRV/DEMO/<year>/<serial>` immediately
on submission.

An acknowledgement is issued **within two working days**. The acknowledgement
states the reference number, the officer assigned, and the expected date of
first response. Anonymous grievances are recorded for trend analysis but cannot
be escalated, because no remedy can be communicated back.

## 4. Stage 2 — Departmental resolution

The grievance is routed to the Head of the concerned department. The department
must record a first response within **seven working days** of acknowledgement.
Where a resolution requires action by another department, the receiving Head
records a handover note against the reference number rather than closing it.

A grievance may be closed at this stage only if the student records acceptance
of the outcome. Silence is not acceptance.

## 5. Stage 3 — Escalation to the Grievance Redressal Committee

If the student is dissatisfied, or the departmental stage exceeds fifteen
working days without a first response, the grievance escalates automatically to
the Grievance Redressal Committee. Automatic escalation does not require the
student to file again; the reference number carries forward with its full
history.

The Committee meets at least once a month during term. It may call the student
and the concerned staff member, may seek written submissions, and records its
finding with reasons.

## 6. Stage 4 — Appeal

An appeal against the Committee's finding lies with the Principal and must be
filed within **ten working days** of the finding being communicated. The
Principal's decision closes the matter internally.

## 7. Records and reporting

All grievances, at whatever stage, are recorded in the grievance register with
the reference number, date of registration, category, stage, and date of
closure. A quarterly summary reporting volume, category distribution and median
resolution time is placed before the IQAC. Individual grievances are not named
in that summary.

## 8. Timeline summary

| Stage | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acknowledgement issued | 2 working days |
| 2 | Departmental first response | 7 working days |
| 2 | Automatic escalation if no response | 15 working days |
| 3 | Committee finding | Next scheduled meeting |
| 4 | Appeal to Principal | Within 10 working days of finding |
