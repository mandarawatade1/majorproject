## Notes

- **Friday** is reserved for major project work. No lectures are scheduled.
  Project reviews per DEMO/CE/2026/07 occupy the Friday afternoon slot on review
  weeks.
- **Laboratory sessions** are two hours and are conducted in batches. Batch
  allocation is published separately on the department notice board.
- **Elective I and Elective II** slots are common across divisions so that
  students from different divisions can take the same elective.

## Venue

Lectures in the department lecture rooms; laboratories in CE Lab 1 through CE
Lab 4 as allocated per batch. Venue changes are announced on the department
notice board and take effect from the date stated, not retroactively.

## Changes

Timetable changes during the term are issued as amendments with a date of
effect. An amendment supersedes this grid only for the slots it names. Where a
session is cancelled for staff leave, the adjustment follows DEMO/WIKI/HR/008 —
a substitute or a rescheduled slot, not simply a cancelled session.

## Attendance

Attendance is recorded per session against this grid. A student attending a
rescheduled session is marked against the original slot, which is why
adjustments must be recorded rather than handled informally.
