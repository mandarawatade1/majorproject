The vision and mission of Pillai College of Engineering (PCE) are as follows:

- To develop professional engineers with respect for the environment and to make them responsible citizens, both from a local and global perspective. This objective is fulfilled through quality education, practical training, research, entrepreneurship, and interaction with industries and social organizations. [S1]
- The vision of the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) at Pillai College of Engineering is to inculcate the entrepreneurship culture among students, faculty members, and alumni. It aims to train a diverse population of students to become responsible professionals or entrepreneurs in a technical arena. It will further train the faculty members and students to contribute in nation-building through an entrepreneurial mindset. [S2]
