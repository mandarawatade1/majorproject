## Circular DEMO/CE/2026/13

**To:** Students entering Semester V, Computer Engineering
**From:** Department of Computer Engineering
**Subject:** Professional Elective I — selection window and capacities

> Course titles and capacities below are **illustrative**.

## Window

Selection opens **3 July 2026, 10:00** and closes **8 July 2026, 17:00**.
Selection is through the student portal under Academics → Electives, and is
available only to students who have completed registration. An incomplete
registration cannot select an elective.

## Allocation rule

Allocation is **first-come within capacity**, by the timestamp of a completed
selection. It is not by academic standing, and it is not by preference order —
a single selection is made, not a ranked list.

When a course fills, it disappears from the available list. Students are
advised to have a second choice in mind before opening the portal rather than
deciding at that moment.

## Prerequisites

The portal enforces prerequisites. A student who has not cleared a prerequisite
cannot select that elective, and no exception is available through the portal.
Where a student believes the prerequisite record is wrong, that is an academic
office matter and must be raised before the window opens, not during it.

A concurrent prerequisite means the course is taken in the same semester and is
enforced at registration rather than at selection.

## Minimum viable enrolment

An elective attracting fewer than fifteen selections at the close of the window
will not run. Students who selected it are informed on 9 July and are allocated
to a remaining course with available capacity, taking their stated alternative
into account where one was recorded.

## Changes

A change of elective after the window closes requires the Head of Department's
approval and is granted only where capacity exists and no teaching has yet been
missed. Changes are not permitted after the second week of term.

## Queries

To the elective coordinator during consultation hours. Queries about which
elective is "easier" will not be answered; queries about content and
prerequisites will be.
