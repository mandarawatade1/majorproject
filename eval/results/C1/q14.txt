The attendance policy for students at Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel, is structured to ensure continuous monitoring and appropriate follow-up. Here are the key points:

- Attendance is computed per course, not as an overall average. A student can be at risk in a specific course even if their overall attendance is high. [S5]
- A student's return to academic activity after a medical emergency is coordinated with the academic office to ensure missed assessments and attendance are handled formally. [S1]
- Industrial visits and field trips have a strict attendance policy with multiple check-ins to ensure no student is missed. [S3]
- Rescheduled sessions are marked against the original slot to maintain accurate records. [S2]
- Any incident, no matter how minor, is reported to the Head of Department on the same day. [S3]
- Attendance is computed from the recorded sessions in the registration grid, excluding sessions cancelled by the institute. [S4]

These policies aim to maintain high standards of attendance and address any issues promptly. [S4]
