## 1. Purpose

To define how an industrial visit or field trip is proposed, approved,
conducted and reported, and the safety and consent requirements that apply.

## 2. Lead time

A proposal is submitted at least **twenty-one working days** before the
intended date. Visits involving overnight travel require **thirty-five working
days**. The lead time exists because consent collection and transport
contracting cannot be compressed, not as bureaucracy for its own sake.

## 3. Supervision ratio

| Group size | Minimum accompanying faculty |
|---|---|
| Up to 30 students | 2 |
| 31 to 60 students | 3 |
| 61 to 90 students | 4 |
| Above 90 | Split into separate visits |

Where the group includes students of more than one gender, accompanying
faculty must include at least one member of each gender. A visit that cannot
meet the ratio is postponed rather than run short-staffed.

## 4. Proposal

Form DEMO/ACAD/F12 records: destination and host organisation, date and
duration, academic justification mapped to a course outcome, expected student
count, accompanying faculty, transport arrangement, and the per-student cost.

Host confirmation must be attached. A proposal citing a host that has not yet
agreed is returned; departments have previously discovered a refusal two days
before departure, which is the reason for this rule.

## 5. Consent and records

Every participating student submits a consent form signed by a parent or
guardian where the student is under eighteen, and an acknowledgement of the
conduct expectations in all cases. Consent forms are held by the department
until the visit is reported, then archived for one year.

A student who has not submitted consent does not travel. This is not
negotiable on the day of departure.

## 6. Before departure

The organising faculty member carries: the participant list with contact
numbers, the consent forms, a first-aid kit, and the host's contact details.
The department office holds a copy of the participant list.

Students are briefed on conduct, safety at the host site, and the reporting
time and place for return travel.

## 7. During the visit

Attendance is taken at departure, on arrival at the host site, before return
departure, and on arrival back at campus. Four counts, not two — a student
missing at the third count is discovered at the host site rather than at the
campus gate.

Any incident, however minor, is reported to the Head of Department the same
day rather than in the written report a week later.

## 8. Reporting

Within **seven working days**: a report of what was observed, the attendance
record, photographs, and a short student feedback summary. Reimbursement of
expenses is released against this submission.

## 9. Records

The departmental activity register holds one row per visit with reference
number, date, host, participants and mapped course outcome, and feeds the
annual report.
