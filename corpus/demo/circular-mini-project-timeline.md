## Circular DEMO/CE/2026/07

**To:** All students of Semester VI, Computer Engineering
**From:** Mini-Project Coordinator, Department of Computer Engineering
**Subject:** Mini-project submission and review timeline

All Semester VI students are informed of the following schedule for the
mini-project. Dates are firm. Requests for individual extensions will not be
entertained except on documented medical grounds approved by the Head of
Department.

## Schedule

| Milestone | Date | Submitted to |
|---|---|---|
| Group formation and topic proposal | 20 January 2026 | Mini-project coordinator |
| Topic approval communicated | 27 January 2026 | — |
| Review I: problem statement and literature | 17 February 2026 | Review panel |
| Review II: design and partial implementation | 24 March 2026 | Review panel |
| Draft report to supervisor | 14 April 2026 | Supervisor |
| Similarity check initiated | 21 April 2026 | Supervisor |
| Final report and demonstration | 5 May 2026 | Review panel |

## Group composition

Groups are of three or four students. Groups of two are permitted only where an
odd number remains after allocation, and require the coordinator's approval.
Cross-division groups are not permitted this semester because review panels are
scheduled division-wise.

## Topic approval

A topic proposal must state the problem, the intended approach, the tools to be
used, and what will be demonstrable at each review. Proposals that restate a
published tutorial or reproduce an existing open-source project without stated
extension will be returned.

Two groups in the same division may not work on the same topic. Where duplicate
proposals are received, priority follows submission timestamp.

## Reviews

Each review is fifteen minutes: ten minutes of presentation, five of questions.
Marks are recorded by the panel at the end of the session and are not revised
afterwards except by written appeal to the Head of Department within three
working days.

Absence from a review without prior intimation carries zero for that review.
Where intimation is given in advance and accepted, a single make-up slot is
offered in the same week; there is no second make-up.

## Report

Reports follow the departmental template, are checked for similarity per
DEMO/SOP/ACAD/2026/12, and must be within the 10% threshold before final
submission is accepted. Students are advised to initiate the check well before
21 April, as the queue lengthens in the final week.

## Queries

Queries to the mini-project coordinator during the published consultation hours.
Queries about individual topic suitability will not be answered by email; bring
the proposal to consultation.
