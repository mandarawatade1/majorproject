The vision of Pillai College of Engineering (PCE) is to admit, educate, and train a diverse population of students to become responsible professionals or entrepreneurs in a technical arena. It aims to contribute to nation-building through an entrepreneurial mindset [S1, S3, S4]. The mission of PCE is to develop entrepreneurs and make them responsible citizens in technological development from both an Indian and global perspective. This is achieved through quality education, practical training, and interaction with start-up founders [S4].
