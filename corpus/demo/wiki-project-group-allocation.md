## Purpose

How project groups are formed and supervisors allocated for the major project,
and the constraints the allocation must satisfy.

## Group formation

Groups are formed by students, of three or four members, within the same
division. The coordinator allocates unattached students to groups with fewer
than four members, in roll-number order.

A group of two is permitted only where the division's numbers leave no
alternative, and requires the Head of Department's approval. Groups of five are
not permitted under any circumstances, because the review format cannot
meaningfully assess five contributions in fifteen minutes.

## Supervisor load

| Supervisor category | Maximum groups |
|---|---|
| Professor | 4 |
| Associate Professor | 4 |
| Assistant Professor | 3 |
| Assistant Professor in first year of service | 2 |

A supervisor already guiding M.Tech dissertations counts each dissertation as
one group against the limit.

## Allocation procedure

1. Groups submit three preferred supervisors in order, with a one-line reason
   for the first preference.
2. Supervisors indicate the domains they will accept, and may decline a specific
   group with a recorded reason.
3. The coordinator runs the allocation: first preferences are honoured where
   load permits, in order of the group's topic-proposal submission timestamp.
4. Groups not allocated on first preference move to second, then third.
5. Groups still unallocated are placed by the coordinator against remaining
   capacity, matching domain where possible.

## Tie-breaking

Where two groups hold the same first preference and only one slot remains, the
earlier topic-proposal timestamp wins. Where timestamps are identical to the
minute, the coordinator decides and records the reason. Ties are not broken by
academic standing, deliberately.

## Changing supervisor

A change after allocation requires the written agreement of the outgoing
supervisor, the incoming supervisor, and the Head of Department. Changes are not
permitted after Review I, because the reviews assess progress against a plan
agreed with a specific supervisor.

## Domain mismatch

A group allocated outside its topic domain may request re-allocation within
seven days of the allocation being published. After that window, the group works
with the allocated supervisor and, if needed, an additional co-supervisor is
named for domain input.

## Records

The allocation register records group members, topic, supervisor, allocation
round and any exception. It is published to the department notice board and is
the reference for review panel constitution.
