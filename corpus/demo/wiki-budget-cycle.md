## The cycle

| Stage | When | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Departmental proposals invited | December | Administrative office |
| Proposals submitted | January | Heads of Department |
| Consolidation and review | February | Finance committee |
| Approval | March | Governing body |
| Financial year begins | April | — |
| Mid-year review | September | Finance committee |
| Close and reconciliation | March | Administrative office |

The mid-year review is where unspent allocations are identified and may be
reallocated. A department that has not spent against a head by September is
likely to lose part of it, which is why the review exists rather than waiting
until March to discover the same thing with no time to act.

## Head codes

Illustrative structure, not the institute's real chart of accounts:

| Code | Head |
|---|---|
| DEMO-EQ | Equipment, capital |
| DEMO-CN | Consumables |
| DEMO-MT | Maintenance and repair |
| DEMO-TR | Staff training and development |
| DEMO-ST | Student activities |
| DEMO-LB | Library and subscriptions |
| DEMO-CT | Contingency |

## The rule people get wrong

**Heads are not interchangeable.** An allocation under DEMO-EQ cannot be spent
on consumables because the equipment purchase fell through. Reallocation
between heads requires a written request at the mid-year review, approved by
the finance committee. Spending across heads and reconciling afterwards is the
most common audit finding.

## Raising a spend

Every spend references a head code and a budget line on the requisition — see
DEMO/SOP/FIN/2026/06. A requisition without a head code is returned rather than
assigned one by the office, because guessing the head is how misallocation
starts.

## Commitments versus spend

An approved purchase order commits the budget even before payment. The
available balance shown to a department is allocation minus commitments minus
spend. Departments that track only payments believe they have more available
than they do, typically in February.

## Carry-forward

Unspent allocation does not carry forward into the next financial year except
where a committed purchase order straddles the year end, in which case the
commitment carries and the balance does not.

## Contingency

DEMO-CT is for genuinely unforeseen expenditure. It is not a top-up for a head
that was under-estimated at proposal time; that is what the mid-year review
handles. Repeated contingency draws by one department are queried at the review.

## Records

The administrative office holds allocation, commitments, spend and
reallocations per head per department, and issues a monthly statement. A
department disputing a figure should raise it against that month's statement
rather than at year end.
