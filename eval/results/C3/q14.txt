The attendance policy for students at Pillai College of Engineering, New Panvel, is as follows:

- Attendance is computed per course, not as an overall average [S5]. This means that a student can have an overall attendance of 85% but still be at risk in a course with 52% attendance.
- The thresholds for attendance are as follows:
  - 75% and above: Satisfactory, no action required [S5].
  - 65% to 74.9%: First warning, with a written warning and the mentor informed [S5].
  - 55% to 64.9%: Second warning, with the parent/guardian informed and a mentor meeting [S5].
  - Below 55%: Detention risk, referred to the detention committee [S5].

These policies ensure that the institution can identify and address specific course attendance issues early on.
