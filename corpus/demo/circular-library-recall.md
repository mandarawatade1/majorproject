## Circular DEMO/LIB/2026/09

**To:** All students and faculty
**From:** Librarian
**Subject:** Recall of borrowed material and semester-end clearance

## Recall

All books, reference material and project reports issued to students are
recalled by **30 April 2026**. Faculty issues are recalled by **7 May 2026**.
Material required beyond these dates must be re-issued in person after the
recall; renewal through the portal is disabled during the clearance period.

## Overdue

The overdue charge applies per item per day after the recall date. Overdue
charges are not waived on the grounds that the borrower was on leave, on
industrial visit, or unaware of the recall; the recall is published here, on the
portal, and by email to every borrower with an item on loan.

An item overdue by more than thirty days is treated as lost and is charged at
replacement cost plus processing.

## Clearance

Library clearance is required before: collection of the semester result card,
issue of a bonafide or transfer certificate, and registration for the following
semester. Clearance is granted when the borrower has no item on loan and no
outstanding charge.

Clearance is recorded against the borrower's identity number and is visible on
the portal within one working day. Students are advised to verify clearance
before approaching the examination office rather than after.

## Reading room during clearance

The reading room and the digital resources remain open through the clearance
period and through the examination period. Only issue and renewal are suspended.
Reference-only material continues to be consultable within the library.

## Lost material

A borrower who has lost an item should report it before the recall date rather
than allowing it to run overdue. Reporting early stops the overdue accrual at
the date of report. Replacement may be made by supplying an identical edition
where one is available, or by paying replacement cost.

## Queries

Queries at the circulation desk during working hours, or through the library
section of the student portal.
