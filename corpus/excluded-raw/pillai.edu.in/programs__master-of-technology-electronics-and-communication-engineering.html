    M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication Program Overview

    The M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication provides a comprehensive academic foundation with industry-relevant curriculum, practical learning, and career-focused education designed for professional success.

    The M.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering provides advanced knowledge in communication systems, embedded systems, VLSI design, and signal processing, with exposure to emerging technologies like AI, IoT, and 5G, combining theoretical concepts with practical and research-oriented learning.

    Graduates will analyze and design communication and electronic systems, develop embedded and VLSI solutions, apply signal processing techniques, utilize AI and IoT technologies, and conduct research to solve complex engineering problems effectively.

    Program Highlights

    The M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication offers industry exposure, modern infrastructure, expert faculty, internships, skill development, and holistic campus learning experiences.

    Industry-Oriented Curriculum

    Curriculum aligned with latest industry trends, focusing on practical skills and real-world applications.

    State-of-the-Art Laboratories

    Advanced labs with modern tools for hands-on learning in embedded systems, IoT, and communication.

    Research and Innovation Focus

    Encourages research, publications, and innovative project development in emerging technologies.

    Research Areas

    Wireless & 5G Communication

    Advanced study of wireless systems, 5G/6G technologies, and high-speed data communication networks.

    Signal & Image Processing

    Processing, analysis, and interpretation of signals and images for real-world applications.

    VLSI & Embedded Systems

    Design and development of integrated circuits and embedded systems for efficient hardware solutions.

    Artificial Intelligence & ML

    Application of AI and machine learning techniques in communication and electronic systems.

    Internet of Things (IoT)

    Development of connected smart devices and systems for automation and data-driven applications.

    Software Defined Networks

    Programmable networking for efficient and flexible management of communication systems.

    Robotics & Automation

    Design and control of intelligent robotic systems for industrial and real-world automation.

    Major Specialization Tracks

    The M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication offers specialized career tracks in high-demand domains, helping students build expertise, innovation skills, and professional competence. Students may select a M.Tech ECE specialization to develop deep expertise. Elective subjects will be based on the chosen track of M.Tech ECE course.

    RF and Microwave

    Design and analysis of RF circuits, antennas, microwave systems, and high-frequency communication technologies.

    Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

    Development of intelligent systems using data-driven models, deep learning, and real-time decision-making algorithms.

    IoT and Robotics

    Design of connected smart devices and robotic systems for automation, sensing, and real-world applications.

    Syllabus

    The M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication syllabus integrates core fundamentals, emerging technologies, interdisciplinary subjects, and practical training aligned with current industry trends.The M.Tech ECE syllabus is designed to build essential knowledge, practical skills, and leadership qualities for success in today’s evolving professional world

    Structured across core courses, DLOC-based electives, skill labs, and multi-phase dissertation, the curriculum integrates communication, networks, AI, VLSI, and IoT domains with hands-on learning, industry exposure, internship, and research-driven project work.

    Program Outcomes

    The M.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication helps students achieve strong technical knowledge, critical thinking, professional skills, research aptitude, and global career readiness.

    • An ability to independently carry out research /investigation and development work to solve practical problems.
    • An ability to write and present a substantial technical report/document.
    • Students achieve higher-level expertise in specialization beyond undergraduate curriculum, demonstrating strong conceptual and practical proficiency.

    A valid score in PULSE, GATE or other recognised national/state-level tests is required. These exams are used for merit-based selection into the programme.

    M.Tech ECE Application Process:

    Applications are submitted online through the university portal. Shortlisted candidates may be called for counselling or personal interaction as per university guidelines.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does the program include practical and industry-oriented learning?

    Yes, the curriculum includes lab courses, Industry 4.0 exposure, internships, and multi-phase dissertation to ensure hands-on and industry-relevant learning.

    Are internships a part of the curriculum?

    Yes, students must complete an internship or relevant certification in Semester III to gain real-world experience.

    What career opportunities are available after completing this program?

    Graduates can pursue roles in embedded systems, communication, VLSI design, AI/ML, networking, IoT, or continue with research and Ph.D. programs.

    What are the key specializations offered in this program?

    The program offers flexible specializations through DLOC electives in Communication Systems, VLSI, Signal Processing, AI/ML, IoT, Networks, and emerging areas like 5G and Edge Computing.
