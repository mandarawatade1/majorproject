## Purpose

This page explains what actually happens when a student clicks Register on the
portal, so that staff handling registration queries understand where a student
is stuck rather than simply re-trying the same action.

## The five gates

Registration passes five gates in order. A student blocked at gate 2 will see a
generic failure; the useful diagnostic is knowing which gate.

**Gate 1 — Programme standing.** The student must be on an active programme
record for the relevant semester. A student returning after a break, or
transferred between divisions, will fail here until the programme record is
updated by the academic office.

**Gate 2 — Financial clearance.** No outstanding dues on the fee record. This is
the most common block. The fee record updates overnight, so a payment made today
usually clears the gate tomorrow, not immediately.

**Gate 3 — Library clearance.** No item on loan past recall and no outstanding
charge, per DEMO/LIB/2026/09.

**Gate 4 — Academic eligibility.** The student satisfies the progression rule
for entry to the semester. Where a student is carrying backlogs beyond the
permitted number, this gate blocks and cannot be overridden by the portal; it
requires an academic decision.

**Gate 5 — Course selection validity.** The selected courses must satisfy credit
minimums and maximums, must not clash in the timetable, and electives must have
space remaining.

## Why "try again later" is usually wrong advice

Gates 1 and 4 do not resolve with time; they need a record change or an academic
decision. Telling a student to retry wastes days. Gates 2 and 3 do resolve with
time once the underlying action is taken. Establish which gate before advising.

## Elective allocation

Electives are allocated first-come within the published window, subject to
capacity. When an elective fills, students holding it in an incomplete
registration do not hold a reservation — capacity is counted on completed
registrations only. This is deliberate, and it is why students are advised to
complete registration in one sitting rather than leaving it half done.

## Late registration

The late window opens after the regular window closes and carries a late fee.
Registration after the late window requires the Head of Department's approval
and is recorded as an exception in the registration report.

## Reconciliation

At the close of registration, the academic office reconciles the registration
list against the enrolled-students list. Students enrolled but not registered
are contacted individually. This reconciliation is what catches students stuck
at gate 1 or gate 4 who simply gave up.
