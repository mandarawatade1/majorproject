    BSc Fashion Design Program Overview

    The BSc Fashion Design provides a comprehensive academic foundation with industry-relevant curriculum, practical learning, and career-focused education designed for professional success.

    The program develops creative and technical competence in apparel, textiles, and fashion technology through structured design education, hands-on studios, digital tools, and applied research, preparing students for diverse professional and academic pathways.

    Students learn fashion illustration, garment construction, textile science, surface design, CAD-based fashion tools, portfolio development, merchandising principles, and sustainable design practices through studio work, workshops, research exposure, and guided industry-oriented projects.

    The BSc Fashion Design syllabus integrates core fundamentals, emerging technologies, interdisciplinary subjects, and practical training aligned with current industry trends.The BSc Fashion Design syllabus is designed to build essential knowledge, practical skills, and leadership qualities for success in today’s evolving professional world

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What are the higher study options after a Bachelor of Interior Design?

    Graduates can pursue M.Des in Interior Design, PG Diploma in Interior Architecture, MBA in Design Management, or apply for global programs abroad by qualifying relevant entrance exams.

    What is the difference between studying Fashion Design at Pillai University vs NIFT or NID?

    Both offer good career opportunities. Pillai provides more personalized attention, practical training, and industry exposure, while NIFT and NID are nationally reputed institutes with wider recognition and broader exposure.

    What specializations are available in Bachelor of Fashion Design?

    Apparel Design, Textile Design, Hand Embroidery, Pattern Making & Garment Construction, Fashion Marketing, Fashion Illustration, Portfolio Development, Fashion CAD, Sustainable Fashion, Luxury Brand Management.

    What is the fee structure and scholarship availability for Bachelor of Fashion Design at Pillai University?

    The fee for Bachelor of Fashion Design is around Rs. 1,00,000. Scholarships are available based on various eligibility criteria.

    What tools, software, and skills are covered in Bachelor of Fashion Design?

    Students learn garment construction (basic to advanced), pattern making, fashion CAD, Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, digital print development, draping, embroidery, traditional textiles, weaving, knitting, natural dyeing, and techniques like screen printing, batik, macrame, and crochet.

    Is Fashion Design a good career choice in India in 2026?

    Yes, Fashion Design is a strong career choice in 2026, with growth driven by sustainable fashion, D2C brands, luxury retail, and digital technologies. The industry offers opportunities in design, marketing, retail, and entrepreneurship, with emerging roles in digital fashion, AI-based design, and sustainable textiles creating future-ready careers.

    What is the average salary after a Bachelor of Fashion Design?

    Entry-level salaries typically range from ₹3–5 LPA, depending on skills, internships, and the company. With experience, salaries increase at mid and senior levels based on expertise and role.

    What are the top career roles after a Bachelor of Fashion Design?

    Fashion Designer, Fashion Stylist, Fashion Merchandiser, Garment Technologist, Production Manager, Fashion Buyer, Fashion Journalist, Fashion Illustrator, Costume Designer, Visual Merchandiser, Fashion Consultant, Fashion Entrepreneur, Retail Manager, Trend Forecaster, Apparel Quality Controller, Pattern Maker, Fashion Marketing Executive.

    Does the Bachelor of Fashion Design program include internships and projects?

    Yes, the program includes internships, live design briefs, fashion show production, and a final portfolio presentation.

    What are the higher study options after a Bachelor of Fashion Design?

    Graduates can pursue M.Des, PG Diploma (NIFT), MBA in Fashion or Luxury Management, or specialized master’s in textile, apparel, styling, communication, or sustainable fashion. They can also apply to international institutes in cities like Milan, London, or New York for advanced fashion and design programs.
