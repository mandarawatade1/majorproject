> Deliberately contains **no telephone numbers**. Real emergency contacts must
> never be represented by placeholder values in a system that answers
> questions. Where a number would appear, this document says who to ask
> instead.

## 1. Immediate response

Anyone present:

1. Ensure the scene is safe before approaching.
2. Send a second person to the nearest staff member or the administrative
   office. Do not leave the affected person alone if avoidable.
3. Do not move a person who may have a spinal or limb injury unless leaving
   them creates a greater danger.
4. If trained, provide first aid within the limits of that training. If not
   trained, do not attempt techniques learned informally.

## 2. Who is informed, and in what order

1. The nearest available staff member.
2. The administrative office, which holds the current emergency contact list.
3. The Head of the concerned department.
4. The student's parent or guardian, from the record held by the academic
   office.

The administrative office holds the authoritative contact list. It is not
reproduced in departmental documents, because a stale copy of an emergency
number is worse than no copy.

## 3. First-aid points

First-aid kits are located at the administrative office, each laboratory
block, the library, and the sports facility. Kits are checked monthly and
after any use; the checking record is held with the administrative office.

The lab assistant or facility in-charge is responsible for reporting a used or
incomplete kit the same day.

## 4. Transport to hospital

Where transport is needed, the administrative office arranges it and records
the destination facility. A staff member accompanies the student and stays
until a parent, guardian or the emergency contact arrives.

## 5. Recording

An incident report is filed within **twenty-four hours** stating: date, time
and location, what happened, who responded, what was done, and the outcome.
The report is factual. It records observations, not conclusions about cause or
fault.

Incident reports are reviewed quarterly to identify recurring hazards. A
location generating repeated incidents is a facilities problem, not a run of
bad luck.

## 6. Follow-up

The student's return to academic activity is coordinated with the academic
office so that missed assessments follow DEMO/EXAM/2026/03 and missed
attendance follows DEMO/SOP/ACAD/2026/18. The student should not have to
initiate that themselves.

## 7. Training

Staff first-aid refresher training is scheduled annually. Attendance is
recorded, and departments with no trained staff member present during teaching
hours are flagged to the administrative office.
