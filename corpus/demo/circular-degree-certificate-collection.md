## Circular DEMO/EXAM/2026/09

**To:** Graduating students
**From:** Examination Office
**Subject:** Convocation registration and certificate collection

## Registration

Graduating students register for convocation through the portal between
**1 and 20 June 2026**. Registration is required whether or not the student
intends to attend in person, because it also determines how the certificate is
released.

Students not attending select either collection in person on a later date, or
collection by an authorised representative.

## Clearance

Certificates are released only against complete clearance:

| Clearance | Held by |
|---|---|
| Library | Librarian — see DEMO/LIB/2026/09 |
| Laboratory | Department, per equipment issued |
| Accounts | Administrative office |
| Hostel, where applicable | Hostel office |

Clearance status is visible on the portal. Students are advised to verify it in
early June rather than discovering an outstanding item on collection day.

## Collection in person

Bring the identity card and the convocation registration confirmation. Where
the identity card has been surrendered, bring a government photo identity
document.

## Collection by a representative

An authorised representative brings: a signed authorisation from the student, a
copy of the student's photo identity, and the representative's own photo
identity. All three are required. A message from the student is not an
authorisation.

## Duplicate certificates

A duplicate is issued only on an application stating the circumstances, with
the prescribed supporting documentation, and takes considerably longer than the
original. Students are advised to collect the original rather than relying on a
duplicate later.

## Transcripts

Transcripts are a separate application from the certificate and follow the
alumni transcript procedure. Requesting a transcript does not release the
certificate, and collecting the certificate does not produce a transcript.

## Keep the reference

The convocation registration reference is of the form
`CONV/DEMO/2026/<serial>`. Quote it in any query. Queries without a reference
take longer to resolve because the record has to be located by name, and names
are not unique.

## Queries

Examination office during working hours.
