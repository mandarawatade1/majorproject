## Notes

- **Slot 3 on Wednesday** is the monthly mentoring group meeting in the first
  week of each month, and library time otherwise. See DEMO/WIKI/ACAD/037.
- **Elective I** is common across divisions so that students from different
  divisions can take the same course. Capacities and the selection window are in
  DEMO/CE/2026/13.
- **Thursday afternoon** is mini-project work. Review dates are issued
  separately.

## Batches

Laboratory sessions run in three batches, B1 to B3. Batch allocation is
published on the department notice board in the first week and does not change
during the term except by written amendment.

A student attending a laboratory session with the wrong batch is marked absent
for their own batch, since attendance is recorded against the roster.

## Venue

Lectures in the department lecture rooms. CN Lab in CE Lab 2, DBMS Lab in CE
Lab 1, SE Lab in CE Lab 4.

## Amendments

Amendments are issued with a date of effect and supersede this grid only for the
slots they name. A cancelled session is adjusted rather than dropped — see
DEMO/WIKI/HR/008.
