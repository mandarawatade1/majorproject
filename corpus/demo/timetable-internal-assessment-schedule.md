## Reporting

Report to the allotted room **fifteen minutes** before the start. Entry is not
permitted more than fifteen minutes after the start, and no additional time is
given to a student who arrives late.

## Room allocation

Allocation is by roll number range and is posted outside each room on the day.
Sitting in the wrong room means the answer script is collected against the wrong
roster, which is corrected slowly and at the student's cost.

## What to bring

Identity card, and permitted materials only. The permitted-materials list is
printed on each question paper; where a paper says nothing, assume nothing is
permitted. Electronic devices are not permitted in any assessment.

## Absence

A student missing an assessment should read DEMO/EXAM/2026/03 before doing
anything else. The retest application window is **seven working days** from the
missed assessment, and applications outside it are not considered regardless of
the reason.

## Retest window

Retests for both assessments are held in a single consolidated window in the
week following the regular assessment. Retest room allocation is published
separately.

## Amendments

Changes are issued as amendments with a date of effect. Where a date changes,
the amendment states both the old and the new date to avoid ambiguity.
