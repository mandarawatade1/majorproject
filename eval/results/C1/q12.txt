The Computer Engineering department focuses on the following research areas:

- Computer Networks
- Data Mining
- Image Processing
- Cyber Security & Digital Forensics
- Natural Language Processing
- Ubiquitous Computing
- Machine Learning and Data Science [S1]

These areas are also mentioned in another source [S2], which adds:

- Internet of Things
- Big Data Analytics
- Geographic Information Systems

Both sources [S1] and [S2] list similar research areas, with some differences. [S2] includes Internet of Things, Big Data Analytics, and Geographic Information Systems, which are not mentioned in [S1]. [S1] includes Cyber Security & Digital Forensics, which is not explicitly stated in [S2] but is implied in Cyber Security.
