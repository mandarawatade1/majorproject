    Bachelor of Design Program Overview

    The Bachelor of Design provides a comprehensive academic foundation with industry-relevant curriculum, practical learning, and career-focused education designed for professional success.

    The Bachelor of Design program develops creative professionals through interdisciplinary studios, technical training, and human-centered design thinking, preparing students to conceptualize, design, and evaluate innovative products, spaces, and visual systems.

    Students learn design fundamentals, visual communication, digital tools, prototyping, user research, sustainability principles, and system-level thinking to translate ideas into functional, aesthetic, and socially responsive design solutions.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the eligibility criteria for B.Des at Pillai University?

    Candidates must have at least 45% in HSC (any stream). A valid score in PULSE, UCEED, NID DAT, AIEED, or equivalent state CET is required for admission.

    What is the difference between B.Des, B.Arch, and BFA?

    B.Des focuses on product, UI/UX, fashion, and communication design. B.Arch specializes in building and spatial design. BFA emphasizes fine arts like painting and sculpture. For a design career, B.Des is often preferred due to its industry-oriented and versatile nature.

    What specializations are available in B.Des at Pillai University?

    Product Design, Visual Communication, Interior Design, Fashion Design.

    What is the fee structure and scholarship availability for B.Des at Pillai University?

    The fee for B.Des is Rs. 3,50,000. Up to 100% scholarships are available for top performers, along with scholarships for Maharashtra and Navi Mumbai residents.

    What tools and software are taught in the B.Des program?

    Students learn tools like Adobe Suite, Figma, AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion, Rhino, Blender, Miro, and AI-powered design tools.

    What is the average salary after B.Des in India?

    Most B.Des graduates start with salaries around ₹6–10 LPA, with higher packages in tech and product-based companies.

    What are the top career roles after B.Des?

    The top career roles after B.Des. are mostly either a UI/UX Designer or a Product Designer.

    Does the B.Des program at Pillai University cover AI tools and design methodologies?

    Yes, the program covers AI-powered design tools, Design Thinking, and human-centred design methodologies used by top global companies.

    Does the B.Des program include internship, portfolio work, and industry projects?

    Yes, the program includes a mandatory internship in Semester 8, along with portfolio development, live studio projects, and industry collaborations.

    What are the higher study options after B.Des?

    Graduates can pursue M.Des in areas like UI/UX, Product or Interaction Design, opt for an MBA in Design Management, or study MFA/HCI abroad. Short-term certifications from Google, Adobe, or Meta in design and digital tools are also available.
